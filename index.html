Privacy Policy for ScanMate

Effective date: September 24, 2026

ScanMate is a document scanning and PDF utility application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how ScanMate handles information when you use the application.

1. Information You Provide

ScanMate may process documents, photographs, scanned pages, PDF files, text, signatures, barcodes, QR codes, and other content that you choose to scan, import, create, edit, or save.

This content is primarily processed locally on your Android device. ScanMate does not require you to create a ScanMate account in order to use its core document scanning and PDF features.

2. Camera and Document Access

ScanMate may request access to your device camera so that you can scan documents, receipts, identification cards, QR codes, barcodes, and other supported content.

ScanMate may also allow you to select images, PDFs, or other files from your device when you choose to import them.

ScanMate only accesses these files or images when you choose them or when access is required for a feature you explicitly use.

3. Document Storage

Scanned documents, exported PDFs, images, OCR text, signatures, and related files may be stored locally on your device or in locations that you select.

Private Vault content is stored within ScanMate's application storage and is protected using Android Keystore-backed AES-GCM encryption, with hardware-backed protection where supported by the device.

ScanMate does not automatically upload your private documents or Vault contents to a developer-operated cloud storage service.

4. Optical Character Recognition and Machine Learning

ScanMate uses Google ML Kit and Google Play services for features including document scanning, text recognition, language identification, translation, and barcode scanning.

Some ML Kit models are bundled with the application, while other models or components may be downloaded through Google Play services when required.

Document, text-recognition, barcode, and translation features are designed to perform processing on the device where supported.

Google Play services and Google ML Kit may process technical information such as device, application, performance, diagnostic, or service information according to Google's own privacy practices and SDK policies.

5. Translation

ScanMate may use Google ML Kit Translation. Translation models may be downloaded to your device when needed. Text submitted to the local translation feature is processed for the purpose of providing the requested translation.

6. Optional AI Features

Some versions of ScanMate may include optional AI-assisted features such as question, math, or document assistance.

If an online AI feature is enabled, ScanMate will only submit content that you explicitly choose to send for that feature. ScanMate does not automatically send your entire document library or Private Vault contents to an online AI service.

If an online AI service is not configured or available, ScanMate does not silently upload your content in the background.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, ScanMate may request permissions or system access for:

  • Camera access for document and code scanning
  • Access to images or files you choose to import
  • Saving documents, images, or PDFs to device storage
  • Biometric or device credentials for security features such as App Lock or Private Vault
  • Internet connectivity where required by Google Play services, model downloads, or optional online features

ScanMate only uses these permissions for their associated application functionality.

8. Data Sharing

ScanMate does not sell your personal information.

Your documents are not shared with advertisers by ScanMate.

Information may be processed by third-party platform services that are required to provide specific application functionality, such as Google Play services and Google ML Kit. Those services operate according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Analytics and Advertising

ScanMate does not currently include a developer-operated advertising network.

ScanMate does not currently operate its own user-behavior analytics service. However, Google Play services or other integrated Google SDK components may process technical, diagnostic, or performance information as part of providing their services.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Documents and other content stored locally by ScanMate remain on your device until you delete them, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Temporary files and cached processing data may be deleted automatically when processing is complete or when temporary storage is cleared.

You can delete ScanMate documents using the application's document management tools. You may also delete all locally stored application data through Android system settings.

11. Security

ScanMate uses Android security features to help protect locally stored information. Security-sensitive features may use Android Keystore, AES-GCM encryption, biometric authentication, or device credentials where supported.

No method of electronic storage or processing can guarantee absolute security. Users should also maintain appropriate device security such as a screen lock and up-to-date Android security software.

12. Children's Privacy

ScanMate is a general-purpose productivity and document utility application. It is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

13. Third-Party Services

ScanMate may use services provided by Google, including:

  • Google Play services
  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition
  • Google ML Kit Translation
  • Google ML Kit Language Identification
  • Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning

Use of these services may be governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ScanMate features, privacy practices, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
